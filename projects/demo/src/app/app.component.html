Keyboard

  • Tab moves between day cells (and time columns in the week/day views)
  • Enter / Space selects the focused day or time slot
  • ArrowDown on a day cell reaches its first event, ArrowLeft / ArrowRight move between events, ArrowUp / Esc go back
  • Enter on an event triggers eventClick
  • ArrowUp / ArrowDown on a time column move between time slots

Read-only calendar (only eventClick)

Only the day cells with events are tab stops (role="group"): use ArrowDown to reach their events.
